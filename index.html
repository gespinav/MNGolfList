🔒 Your Data & Privacy

Minnesota Public Golf is local-first: everything works on your device with no account. Signing in is optional — it only syncs your bucket list, rounds & handicap privately across your own devices. We never sell your data, show ads, or run third-party trackers.

✅No account needed — played, rounds & handicap live on your device
☁️Optional cloud sync — if you sign in, played / rounds / handicap sync to your own private, access-controlled profile (Google Firebase). Delete it anytime from the account panel.
📵Your course photos never leave this device — they’re never uploaded, even when signed in
🔒If you never sign in, the app makes zero third-party requests (fonts & maps library are self-hosted)
❌We do not sell your data, show ads, profile you, or run analytics/trackers
⛳ Handicap Index

Your USGA Handicap Index is stored only on this device and used to calculate course handicap and net scores alongside your rounds.

Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app will also remove all stored data. Use the export options above to back up your rounds before clearing.
